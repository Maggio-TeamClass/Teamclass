# Health Insurance Benefit

This guide summarizes the company's health insurance benefit for eligible team
members. It is intended as an employee-facing overview and should be kept in
sync with the official plan documents and carrier materials.

## Eligibility

Health insurance is available to regular full-time employees and any dependents
who meet the plan's eligibility requirements.

Eligible dependents may include:

- A legal spouse or domestic partner, where recognized by the plan
- Children up to the plan's dependent age limit
- Other dependents required by applicable law or described in the plan documents

## Enrollment windows

Employees can enroll or change coverage during:

1. **New hire enrollment** - after becoming eligible for benefits.
2. **Open enrollment** - the annual period for selecting coverage for the next
   plan year.
3. **Qualified life events** - events such as marriage, birth or adoption of a
   child, loss of other coverage, divorce, or a change in dependent eligibility.

Qualified life event changes must be submitted within the deadline specified by
the plan administrator.

## Coverage overview

Health insurance plans typically include coverage for:

- Preventive care
- Primary and specialist office visits
- Urgent and emergency care
- Hospital services
- Prescription drugs
- Laboratory and imaging services
- Mental health and substance use care

Specific coverage levels, exclusions, deductibles, copays, coinsurance, and
out-of-pocket maximums are defined by the active plan documents.

## Employee costs

Employee premium contributions are based on the selected plan and coverage tier,
such as employee-only, employee plus spouse or partner, employee plus children,
or family coverage.

Payroll deductions should be reviewed during enrollment to confirm the selected
plan and coverage tier are correct.

## Using the benefit

After enrollment is processed:

1. Review the carrier portal and member ID card.
2. Confirm whether preferred providers and pharmacies are in network.
3. Use preventive care benefits when available.
4. Keep copies of bills, explanations of benefits, and claim communications.
5. Contact the carrier first for claim status, network, or coverage questions.

## Claims and support

For claim questions, employees should contact the insurance carrier using the
phone number or website shown on the member ID card.

For eligibility, enrollment, or payroll deduction questions, employees should
contact the People or Benefits team.

## Compliance note

If this overview conflicts with official plan documents, insurance contracts, or
legally required notices, those official documents control.
